How to order

Counter, token, screen, food.

  1. Choose

    Check the board above the counter.

  2. Pay

    Cash or card at the counter.

  3. Keep your token

    The number is on your receipt.

  4. Collect

    Your number shows on the screen. Show the token at the pick-up window.
